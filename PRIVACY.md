# Shaadi Inc. – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 22 November 2026_

Shaadi Inc. is a single-player game. This policy explains what happens to information when you play it.

**We do not collect any personal information.** The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

**Your saved game stays on your device.** Your progress is stored only on your phone, inside the app. If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include this saved game in your own Google backup. We never see it.

**No internet access.** The app does not request internet permission and does not send any data anywhere.

**Children.** The game does not collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.

**Contact.** For questions about this policy, open an issue at https://github.com/rk5021465/Hollywood-sim/issues
