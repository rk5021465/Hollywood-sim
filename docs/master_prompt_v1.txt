MASTER GAME DEVELOPMENT PROMPT

NEXA — THE TRANSCENDENT SYSTEM

You are my senior game developer, game architect, gameplay programmer, UI/UX designer and technical director.

Your task is to develop a COMPLETE, genuinely playable Android game called:

NEXA — THE TRANSCENDENT SYSTEM

This is a 2D anime open-world RPG and life simulator with a top-down camera, inspired by the progression and quest systems of Solo Leveling and Questism, and the life simulation and management features of modern mobile games.

IMPORTANT: This must be an actual game, NOT a chatbot, NOT a text-only adventure, and NOT a visual novel.

The game must have:

- Playable characters.
- Actual movement and exploration.
- Animated NPCs.
- Interactive environments.
- Real menus and buttons.
- Working quests and rewards.
- A functional card collection.
- A smartphone interface.
- A working economy.
- Character progression.
- A story campaign.
- Save and load functionality.
- Android touchscreen controls.

Use Godot 4.x and GDScript.

2. GAME WORLD AND ORIGINAL STORY

The main planet is called NEXA.

Almost every person on Nexa possesses a superpower or quirk.

Society is extremely unequal.

The strongest individuals receive:

- Luxury houses.
- Free premium services.
- Respect and social status.
- Exclusive shopping privileges.
- Special government treatment.

Weak individuals and powerless people are treated poorly and often forced into labour.

The protagonist is PETER PARKER.

Peter is 16 years old at the beginning.

He is powerless and frequently bullied at school.

He is academically intelligent but struggles in physical competitions.

His father was the No. 1 superhero of Nexa and possessed overwhelming physical power and Ultra Instinct.

His mother was the second-strongest superhero.

Peter's father fought ZERO, an overwhelmingly powerful enemy connected to the Dragon Ball universe.

During their final battle, Peter's father defeated Zero at great cost. However, Zero absorbed his power and imprisoned him on another planet.

Zero eventually conquered Nexa.

Peter's mother received government-provided housing because of her reputation and previous status.

Peter grows up without knowing the full truth about his father's fate.

IMPORTANT STORY RULE:
Preserve this storyline and its major events.

Do not randomly rewrite the protagonist's history, replace characters, change the main villain, or remove major story events.

Expand scenes naturally without changing their intended outcome.
