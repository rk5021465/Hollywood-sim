NEXA — THE TRANSCENDENT SYSTEM

COMPLETE ANDROID GAME DEVELOPMENT MASTER PROMPT

You are my professional game development team, senior Godot developer, RPG architect, UI designer, story designer, database engineer and technical director.

Your mission is to create a complete, polished, playable Android game based on my original story.

This game is called:

NEXA — THE TRANSCENDENT SYSTEM.

IMPORTANT: I want an interactive 2D anime story RPG and life simulator.

I DO NOT want:

- A 3D game.
- A free-roaming character game.
- A basic chatbot.
- A plain text-only story.
- A simple visual novel without functional gameplay systems.

I WANT:

A beautiful illustrated interactive anime RPG where the player reads story scenes, sees character portraits and backgrounds, interacts with NPCs, makes choices, manages a smartphone, completes quests, collects ability cards, improves stats, purchases properties, builds relationships and progresses through a massive multiverse storyline.

Use Godot 4.x and GDScript.

PART 2 — ORIGINAL NEXA STORY

IMPORTANT STORY RULE:

Preserve my original storyline.

Do not change the main events, character relationships, important characters, powers, or planned outcomes.

You may add side stories, conversations, school activities, missions and additional characters, but these additions must support the original storyline.

Do not turn the entire game into an investigation about Peter's father.

Do not unnecessarily delay major story events.

The protagonist is PETER PARKER.

He is 16 years old at the beginning.

The story begins on a planet called NEXA.

Almost everyone on this planet possesses powers or quirks.

The strongest people receive:

- Luxury houses.
- Free premium services.
- Respect.
- Special privileges.
- Better treatment.
- Access to exclusive facilities.

Weak people and powerless individuals are treated badly and often forced to work as labourers.

Peter is powerless.

His father was the No. 1 superhero of Nexa and one of the strongest heroes.

His father possessed incredible physical power comparable to Saitama and Ultra Instinct.

His mother was the second-strongest superhero.

The main enemy is ZERO, an overwhelmingly powerful villain connected to the Dragon Ball universe.

Zero fought Peter's father.

Peter's father defeated Zero at the cost of his own apparent death.

However, Zero absorbed his power and imprisoned him on another planet.

Zero conquered Nexa.

Peter's mother was provided government housing because of her former position.

Peter grows up being bullied at school.

He is academically intelligent but loses physical and power competitions.

Peter is initially skinny, powerless and insecure about his appearance.
